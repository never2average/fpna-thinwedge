# Code of Conduct

ThinWedge uses the Contributor Covenant as the standard for community conduct.
Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and maintainer
spaces is expected to help keep the project respectful, focused, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Avoid sharing private information, credentials, customer data, or other
  sensitive material in public project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or maintainer decisions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make contributors feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block users, or
take other proportionate action when this code is violated. Security-sensitive
or privacy-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of public
issue threads.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers through
the security reporting channel in `SECURITY.md`.
